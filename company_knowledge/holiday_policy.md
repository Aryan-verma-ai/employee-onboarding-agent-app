# Company Holiday Policy

The company publishes an official list of public and festive holidays every year in December for the upcoming calendar year.

## 1. Annual Public Holidays
Employees are entitled to 10 declared paid public holidays per calendar year. These include national celebrations and major festive occasions:
1. Republic Day (January 26)
2. Holi (March)
3. Eid-ul-Fitr (Variable)
4. Independence Day (August 15)
5. Mahatma Gandhi Jayanti (October 2)
6. Dussehra / Vijayadashami (October)
7. Diwali (Deepavali) (October/November)
8. Guru Nanak Jayanti (November)
9. Christmas Day (December 25)
10. State / Regional Holiday (Notified per location)

## 2. Optional / Restricted Holidays
- In addition to the 10 fixed declared holidays, employees may select up to 2 optional restricted holidays per year from the corporate calendar for cultural or religious celebrations.
- Optional holiday requests should be submitted at least 1 week in advance via the HRMS portal.

## 3. Work on Public Holidays
- Customer-facing and mission-critical operations teams working on public holidays receive equivalent compensatory time off (Comp-Off) or premium compensation as per statutory regulations.
