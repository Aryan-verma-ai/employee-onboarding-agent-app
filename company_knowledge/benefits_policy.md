# Company Employee Benefits Policy

We offer a comprehensive benefits package designed to support our employees' physical health, mental wellbeing, financial security, and ongoing professional growth.

## 1. Group Medical Health Insurance (Mediclaim)
- **Coverage**: ₹5,00,000 per family per policy year (floater basis).
- **Covered Dependents**: Employee, legal spouse, and up to 2 dependent children (up to age 25).
- **Features**: Cashless hospitalization across network hospitals, pre/post-hospitalization expenses, daycare treatments, and maternity coverage as per policy terms.
- **TPA & E-Cards**: Health insurance e-cards and TPA portals are activated within 14 days of employee creation.

## 2. Group Personal Accident Insurance (GPA)
- Coverage up to 3 times the employee's annual Cost-to-Company (CTC) against permanent or partial disability resulting from accidental injuries.

## 3. Group Term Life Insurance (GTL)
- Life insurance coverage equal to 3 times annual CTC provided to nominated beneficiaries in the event of an employee's untimely demise.

## 4. Annual Executive Health & Wellness Checkup
- Complimentary annual diagnostic screening covering full blood profile, lipid profile, liver/kidney function tests, and physician consultation at partner diagnostic chains.

## 5. Learning & Professional Development Allowance
- An annual budget of ₹25,000 is available to every confirmed employee for professional certification examinations (e.g. Azure, AWS, Kubernetes), technical books, conference registrations, or educational subscriptions.
- Claims are submitted through the expense portal with GST invoices.

## 6. Employee Assistance Program (EAP)
- Confidential 24/7 mental wellness counseling, financial guidance, and psychological support services available free of charge for employees and immediate family members.
