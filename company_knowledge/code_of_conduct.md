# Company Code of Conduct, Ethics and POSH Policy

This document defines the ethical and professional standards expected of every member of the organization.

## 1. Equal Opportunity & Inclusivity
- We maintain a workplace free from harassment, bias, and discrimination. Decisions regarding hiring, compensation, promotion, and project assignments are made purely based on qualifications, merit, and performance.

## 2. Prevention of Sexual Harassment (POSH)
- **Statutory Framework**: Formulated in strict compliance with the Sexual Harassment of Women at Workplace (Prevention, Prohibition and Redressal) Act, 2013.
- **Internal Complaints Committee (ICC)**: A dedicated ICC headed by a senior woman leader handles complaints confidentially.
- **Resolution Timeline**: Formal inquiries commence within 7 business days of grievance filing. Retaliation against complainants or witnesses is met with immediate termination.

## 3. Conflicts of Interest & Dual Employment
- **Moonlighting**: Engaging in outside commercial employment, freelancing for competitors, or operating an undisclosed business without prior written consent from HR is strictly prohibited.
- **Gifts & Gratuities**: Employees may not accept personal gifts, hospitality, or gratuities exceeding ₹2,000 from vendors or clients.

## 4. Whistleblower Protection
- Employees can report illegal, unethical, or fraudulent behavior confidentially without fear of retaliation via `ethics@company.com` or the anonymous reporting portal.

## 5. Disciplinary Process
- Violations of the Code of Conduct are investigated by HR and senior management, resulting in actions ranging from formal warnings to termination of employment with cause.
