# Company Payroll and Compensation Information

This policy outlines the salary processing timeline, tax deductions, statutory contributions, and reimbursement procedures.

## 1. Salary Disbursement Schedule
- **Pay Day**: Monthly net salary is credited directly to the employee's registered bank account on the **last working day of each calendar month**.
- **Salary Slip**: Monthly payslips are generated and accessible via the self-service HRMS portal within 24 hours of disbursement.

## 2. Bank Account Registration
- All employees must provide valid bank account details (Bank Name, Account Number, and IFSC Code) along with a cancelled cheque or bank statement during onboarding.
- Changes to salary accounts must be submitted to `payroll@company.com` by the 15th of the calendar month to take effect for that month's payroll.

## 3. Statutory Deductions and Contributions
- **Employees' Provident Fund (EPF)**: Statutory 12% deduction from basic salary matched by an equal 12% employer contribution towards EPFO.
- **Tax Deducted at Source (TDS)**: Income tax is deducted as per the Indian Income Tax Act. Employees can declare tax-saving investments (under New or Old tax regimes) through the tax declaration portal.
- **Professional Tax (PT)**: Deducted monthly in accordance with the state government schedules where the employee is stationed.
- **Gratuity**: Payable to employees completing 5 or more continuous years of service as per the Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972.

## 4. Expense Reimbursements
- Business expenses, travel, internet allowance, and learning stipends must be submitted by the 20th of each month for payout in the same month's salary cycle.
