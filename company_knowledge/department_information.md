# Company Department Descriptions & Organizational Structure

Our organization operates five functional departments collaborating to deliver world-class products.

## 1. Engineering
- **Scope**: Product development, cloud architecture, AI models, backend services, mobile applications, Quality Assurance, and DevOps/SRE.
- **Tools**: GitHub, Azure Cloud, Docker, Kubernetes, Python, FastAPI, React.
- **Leadership**: Chief Technology Officer (CTO) & Engineering Directors.

## 2. Human Resources (HR)
- **Scope**: Talent acquisition, employee onboarding, culture, performance management, benefits administration, employee relations, and compliance.
- **Contact**: `hr@company.com`.

## 3. Finance & Accounts
- **Scope**: Corporate financial planning, budget allocation, taxation, payroll accounting, expense audits, and statutory financial reporting.
- **Contact**: `finance@company.com` / `payroll@company.com`.

## 4. Sales & Enterprise Partnerships
- **Scope**: Direct enterprise sales, customer relationships, solutions engineering, client acquisition, and global partnerships.
- **Contact**: `sales@company.com`.

## 5. Operations & Facilities
- **Scope**: IT asset procurement, workstation infrastructure, office facility maintenance, vendor relations, and physical office logistics.
- **Contact**: `operations@company.com`.
