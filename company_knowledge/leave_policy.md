# Company Leave Policy

The company recognizes the importance of work-life balance, health, and personal wellbeing. All leave entitlements are calculated per calendar year (January to December) and pro-rated based on your joining date.

## 1. Annual / Privilege Leave (PL)
- **Entitlement**: 18 working days per calendar year.
- **Accrual**: Accrues at 1.5 days per completed month of service.
- **Carry Forward**: Up to 30 unused PL days can be carried forward to the following calendar year.
- **Notice Period**: Planned annual leave must be requested via the HRMS portal at least 48 hours in advance and approved by your reporting manager.

## 2. Casual & Sick Leave (CL / SL)
- **Entitlement**: 12 working days per calendar year (1 day per month).
- **Purpose**: Available for sudden personal emergencies, family obligations, or acute illness.
- **Medical Certificate**: Required for sick leave extending beyond 3 consecutive working days.
- **Encashment / Carry-Forward**: Casual and sick leave cannot be encashed or carried forward to the next year.

## 3. Maternity Leave
- **Entitlement**: 26 weeks (182 calendar days) of fully paid maternity leave for female employees, compliant with the Maternity Benefit Act.
- **Eligibility**: Employees who have worked for at least 80 days in the 12 months preceding the expected delivery date.
- **Additional Support**: Extended work-from-home options may be requested upon return.

## 4. Paternity Leave
- **Entitlement**: 2 weeks (10 working days) of paid paternity leave for new fathers.
- **Validity**: Must be availed within 6 months of the child's birth or legal adoption.

## 5. Bereavement Leave
- **Entitlement**: Up to 5 consecutive working days of compassionate paid leave in the unfortunate event of the loss of an immediate family member (parents, spouse, siblings, children).

## 6. Compensatory Off (Comp-Off)
- Employees required to work on declared holidays or weekends with prior managerial approval are entitled to compensatory time off, to be availed within 60 days.
