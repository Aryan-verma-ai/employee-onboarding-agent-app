# Company Employee Handbook

Welcome to the company! This Employee Handbook serves as a guide to your employment journey, our core values, working standards, and company resources.

## 1. Company Mission and Culture
Our mission is to build intelligent, enterprise-grade cloud and AI platforms that empower businesses worldwide. We foster an inclusive, collaborative culture centered around transparency, innovation, integrity, and mutual respect.

## 2. Workplace Standards
- **Professional Conduct**: Treat all colleagues, partners, and clients with dignity, fairness, and courtesy.
- **Equal Opportunity**: We are committed to providing equal opportunities to all employees regardless of race, gender, caste, religion, age, sexual orientation, disability, or marital status.
- **Zero Tolerance for Harassment**: Any form of verbal, physical, or sexual harassment is strictly prohibited and subject to immediate disciplinary and statutory action.

## 3. Communication Channels
- **Official Email**: Company email addresses (`@company.com`) are provided for enterprise communications.
- **Enterprise Chat**: Internal messaging tools (Microsoft Teams / Slack) are used for day-to-day team collaboration.
- **HR Helpdesk**: Contact `hr@company.com` for employment queries, policy clarifications, or personal support.
- **IT Support**: Contact `it-support@company.com` for hardware, network, or software assistance.

## 4. Performance & Career Development
- **Probation Period**: Standard probation is 3 months from joining, extendable by up to 3 months based on performance assessment.
- **Annual Reviews**: Formal performance appraisals and compensation reviews occur annually in April.
- **Continuous Learning**: Employees are encouraged to upskill using our annual learning allowance.
