# Company Working Hours, Attendance and Hybrid Work Policy

This policy governs daily working expectations, collaboration timings, attendance marking, and hybrid work arrangements.

## 1. Standard Working Hours
- **Work Week**: Monday through Friday (5-day work week).
- **Daily Hours**: 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM IST (9 hours total), including a 1-hour lunch and refreshment break.
- **Core Collaboration Hours**: 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM IST. During core hours, all team members are expected to be available for sprint ceremonies, customer discussions, and team syncs.

## 2. Hybrid Working Model
- **Office Days**: Employees are expected to work from their designated corporate office at least 3 days per week.
- **Remote Days**: Employees may work remotely up to 2 days per week, subject to project deliverables and manager agreement.
- **Office Amenities**: Ergonomic workstations, cafeterias, high-speed Wi-Fi, and collaboration zones are provided at all office facilities.

## 3. Remote Work Requirements
- When working remotely, employees must have:
  - Reliable high-speed broadband connection (minimum 50 Mbps).
  - Quiet, secure workspace preserving confidentiality.
  - Active enterprise VPN and MFA connected during work sessions.

## 4. Attendance and Time Logging
- Daily check-in: Employees must log in to the HR portal by 9:30 AM to mark attendance.
- Overtime / Late hours: Project teams requiring after-hours deployments must obtain advance approval for compensatory time off.
